## What the configurator does

Material selection records an illustrative construction direction and adjusts the fictional accessory price. It does **not** change structural geometry. Surface texture is selected separately: ribbed grip, cushioned tread, or cork touch. Not all combinations are assumed production-feasible; every combination is available for concept exploration only.

The colors and surface patterns are visual approximations. They do not demonstrate slip resistance or suitability for a particular animal. The catalog's dimensions are demonstration targets, not validated fit dimensions or load ratings.

## Before approving a real product

A qualified product engineering and safety team must define applicable standards, loads, safety margins, and test methods. Do not infer numerical acceptance criteria from the prototype.

- Establish intended animal sizes and mobility needs, vehicle interface, usable slope, and deck dimensions.
- Verify static strength, repeated loading/fatigue, deflection, stability, and support conditions.
- Evaluate traction on dry, wet, muddy, and worn surfaces using methods relevant to paws on an incline.
- Assess pinch, shear, entrapment, sharp-edge, and deployment hazards.
- Validate attachment, stow retention, driving vibration, and access to the cargo floor. The app's lock symbol is conceptual, not proof of a latch design.
- Assess temperature, UV, moisture, corrosion, cleaning chemicals, and service wear.
- Review all constituent materials, recycled-feedstock contaminants, coatings, binders, and adhesives for relevant pet-contact/chewing/ingestion exposure.
- Obtain supplier evidence for sourcing and recycled-content claims. Assess lifecycle impacts before claiming a comparative environmental benefit.
- Define repair, replaceable-part, and end-of-life processes that work in intended markets.

There is no known load capacity, certification, or approved company materials policy represented here.
